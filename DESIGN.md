# Khemet for Terraria: design and roadmap

The Minecraft Khemet mod's ideas, rebuilt for Terraria's 2D, side-on world and its boss-and-progression loop.

## Progression

| Stage | Content |
|---|---|
| Pre-Hardmode | Find the Grand Pyramid (any new world). Loot the Throne Room's cartouche, or craft a **Cursed Cartouche** (8 Sunstone Shards + 4 Gold Bars). Fight **The Forgotten Pharaoh** in the desert. Get the Khopesh, the Pharaoh's Staff, the Scarab Charm, the Ankh of Life. |
| Hardmode | Craft the **Scales of Judgement** (10 Duat Shards + 6 Hallowed Bars, at a Mythril Anvil). Fight **Anubis, the Judge**. Khopesh of Anubis, Staff of Judgement, Amulet of the Duat. |
| Post-Plantera | Craft the **Relic of Ra**. Fight **The Sun Pharaoh**. Solar Khopesh, Staff of the Sun, Disc of Ra. |

## The Grand Pyramid

A cutaway pyramid (see `preview/grand-pyramid.png`): the gate, the Hall of Gates, the Great Gallery up the slope to the Throne Room, the shaft to the Hall of Judgement, the west crypt and the east vault.
The blueprint is generated by `tlib/pyramid.py`; the C# only decodes and places it, so the preview shows exactly what the world generator builds.

## Next (not built)

* **Apep**, the great serpent: a worm boss (head, body, tail segments).
* **Armour sets** with the equip sheets (head, body, legs) and set bonuses: the Pharaoh, Anubis and Sun sets.
* **Mounts**: the war **chariot** (the Minecraft mod's craftable chariot, as a Terraria mount), the **Sacred Scarab** and the **Divine Falcon**.
* **Town NPC**: a scribe who sells maps of the pyramid and hieroglyph blocks.
* **Biome**: the Nile (a river of water through the desert with papyrus and lotus), palms, and an oasis; the **Duat** as a late underworld layer below the pyramid.
* Trophies, relics, boss music, custom walls and furniture (sarcophagus, obelisk, brazier).
* Multiplayer checks, and balance from real play.
